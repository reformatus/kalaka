# Portal

The active registration (set by a secret link or an account's pick), the participant blocks admins place on any page, and the account area.

`Api/` holds `ActiveRegistration` (`current()`: the key the verified cookie names; `followsBadLink()`: the one-shot flag of a link that did not match), `RegistrationCan` (what the registrant can do now: `payCard` and `withdraw`, with `withdrawUnavailable` saying when only the event's registration close stops it) and `CardPaymentView` (the latest card payment request: state and since), `CardAmountsView` (what a card payment may ask for, set only while `can.payCard`: `full`, `advance`, `min`, `max`; `full` and `max` are the amount payable now, `min` is `Payments\Api\CardAmountLimits`' effective minimum, the one rule of the page and of the endpoint, and `advance` is null until the deposit extension fills it), `PayableNow` (the one rule of what is payable now, read by the transfer block and by starting a card payment), `Withdrawal` (the participant's own withdrawal, held to the active registration and its code, over Registrations' `OwnWithdrawal`; `nonceField()` and `nonceValue()` print and compute the form's nonce, bound to the registration's code, `nonceIsValid()` checks it), `WithdrawBadNonce`, `WithdrawStale` and `WithdrawUnavailable` (its refusals: a missing or stale form nonce, a stale page, a status that cannot withdraw), `StatusRead` (`payableNow()`, `of()` and `forActive()`: one registration's `RegistrationStatusView`, composed from the module Apis and the clock; it writes and evaluates nothing), `MoneyRead` (one payer's money table, `ParticipantMoney`, the participant's by default: owed rows `OwedRow` of an `OwedKind`, movements `MovementRow` of a `MovementType` and `MovementDirection`, the totals, and `due` or `overpaid`; one ledger statement of the payer and the names of its owed lines, for every status; `StatusRead` also builds one `OtherPayerMoney` table, with its label, for each other payer that has lines, in Ledger's payer order, for every status but waitlisted) and the view's parts: `WithdrawOffer` (the registrant's own withdrawal as the block shows it: `can.withdraw`, the post's address, the confirm state, a refusal's code, the preview's money (`paid`, `retained`, `surplus`), the form nonce and whether the registration is waitlisted; plain inputs `ParticipantBlocks` builds from the status read and the preview, taken whenever the button shows; a prototype), `Availability`, `ConfirmationView`, `PaymentView` (the totals, the other payers, whether a request awaits an answer, and the due time; the itemised table is `MoneyRead`'s), `PayerAmounts`, `TransferDetails`. `MailMoney` is what a mail reads: the money table and the transfer block in mail markup (`MailMarkup`), and one payment line of the participant as a `MailMovement` (the `MovementRow` and its method's ID), through the same read as the page. `RegistrationStatusView::showsMoney()` is the one rule of whether a money table shows, for the page and for the mail.

`Rest/` holds `WithdrawRoute` (`POST kalaka-portal/v1/withdraw`: JSON `{changed, status}`, or `{allowed, paid, owed_after, retained, surplus}` with `preview`; a plain form post gets a 303 back to the clean same-site address).

At the module root, `WithdrawRefusalCookie` and `WithdrawRefusalState` are the one-shot flash cookie of a refused no-script withdrawal post: its code in the next page request, read and expired at `template_redirect` like the card payment's, and shown once as the status view's `withdrawRefusal`.

At the module root, `CardRefusalCookie` and `CardRefusalState` are the one-shot flash cookie of a refused no-script card payment post: read and expired at `template_redirect` (after canonical redirection), held for that request, and exposed as the status view's `cardRefusal` (a code; the text that shows it is UX's).

At the module root: `LinkHandler` (at `init`: follows a secret link with a 302 that drops the token and sets the signed session cookie `kalaka_active`, or the bad-link flag `kalaka_badlink`; verifies the cookie and clears a bad one; never cached when either cookie is sent; clears the cookie on logout), `Response` and `HttpResponse` (what the handler sends), `ActiveRegistrationState` (the request's resolved state), `ParticipantBlocks` (the renders the blocks and their shortcodes share, never cached, with `portal.css`; the status render builds the `WithdrawOffer`), `StatusMarkup` (the page's markup, with the money table of the owner's design), `MoneyWords` (the words the page and the mail share), `MailMarkup` (the mail look: inline styles, no popover), `LeaveRoute` (`POST kalaka-portal/v1/leave`: the same-site check, the `logged_in` cookie read before `wp_logout()`, the active-registration cookie cleared, a 303 to a same-site `return_url` or the home page), and `LineLabels` (`owedLabelOf()`: the words of an owed row of the money table). An amount's text, `MoneyText`, is Foundation's (`Foundation\Api\Money`). `portal.css` is the blocks' structural stylesheet, served from `src/`.

Entry points: the blocks `Blocks/status/` (`kalaka/status`, "Jelentkezésem állapota", shortcode `[kalaka_status]`, with `view.js`, the status block's front-end script: the withdrawal's confirmation in a WordPress Modal, from data on the start form; the shortcode's render enqueues it too) and `Blocks/transfer/` (`kalaka/transfer`, "Átutalási adatok", shortcode `[kalaka_transfer]`), each a server render with an editor placeholder, and `Blocks/leave/` (`kalaka/leave`, "Kijelentkezés", shortcode `[kalaka_leave]`: one form and one button posting to the leave route, nothing without an active registration or a logged-in account. Every page that shows the button is never cached (`DONOTCACHEPAGE` on each render), so place it with care: in a footer it makes every page uncacheable, and there the no-cache headers are usually already sent, so only page-cache plugins that read `DONOTCACHEPAGE` are stopped, not a proxy or CDN); the `init` handler of the secret link; the route `POST kalaka-portal/v1/withdraw` (`Rest\WithdrawRoute`, on `rest_api_init`), the `template_redirect` reader of the withdrawal refusal cookie (`WithdrawRefusalCookie`); `POST kalaka-portal/v1/card-payment` (`Rest/CardPaymentRoute`: starts a card payment for the active registration from `{return_url?, amount?, popup?}`; it needs the WordPress nonce `kalaka_card_payment` — the form's hidden `_wpnonce`, or the `X-WP-Nonce` header or `_wpnonce` field of a script — checked before anything else, a missing or stale one being `CardNonceInvalid` (`kalaka_card_nonce_invalid`) through the same cookie; `{url}` for JSON, a 303 for a form post, and for a refused form post a 303 back to the clean return address with the refusal's code in the one-shot `kalaka_card_refusal` cookie, which the status read shows once as `cardRefusal`; a refused post with `popup=1` goes to the return endpoint below instead) and `GET kalaka-portal/v1/card-payment/return?to=<status page>[&popup=1]` (the address every order returns to: a 303 to the same-site status page, else home; with `popup=1` the page of `Rest/CardReturnPage`, HTML inside the theme, in a classic theme in the content frame the theme adapter chooses from the closed list `Forms\Api\ContentFrame` (`neutral` by default, or `page`), which core renders, and the adapter's `page_body` classes on the `body`, or bare through the filter `kalaka_card_return_bare`; noindex; it tells the opener `{type: 'kalaka-card-return'}`, tries to close itself and shows a line, a close button and a link to the status page).
