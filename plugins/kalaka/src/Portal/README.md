# Portal

The active registration (set by a secret link or an account's pick), the participant blocks admins place on any page, and the account area.

`Api/` holds `ActiveRegistration` (`current()`: the key the verified cookie names; `followsBadLink()`: the one-shot flag of a link that did not match), `StatusRead` (`of()` and `forActive()`: one registration's `RegistrationStatusView`, composed from the module Apis and the clock; it writes and evaluates nothing), `MoneyRead` (the participant's money table, `ParticipantMoney`: owed rows `OwedRow` of an `OwedKind`, movements `MovementRow` of a `MovementType` and `MovementDirection`, the totals, and `due` or `overpaid`; one ledger statement of the participant payer and the names of its owed lines, for every status), `Withdrawal` (the participant's own withdrawal, held to the active registration and its code, over Registrations' `OwnWithdrawal`), `WithdrawStale` and `WithdrawUnavailable` (its refusals: a stale page, a status that cannot withdraw), `RegistrationCan` (what the registrant can do now: `withdraw`) and the view's parts: `WithdrawOffer` (the registrant's own withdrawal as the block shows it: `can.withdraw`, the post's address, the confirm state, a refusal's code, the preview's money and whether the registration is waitlisted; plain inputs `ParticipantBlocks` builds from the status read and the preview, taken whenever the button shows; a prototype), `Availability`, `ConfirmationView`, `PaymentView`, `PayerAmounts`, `PaymentLine`, `TransferDetails`.

`Rest/` holds `WithdrawRoute` (`POST kalaka-portal/v1/withdraw`: JSON `{changed, status}`, or `{allowed, paid, owed_after}` with `preview`; a plain form post gets a 303 back to the clean same-site address). At the module root: `WithdrawRefusalCookie` and `WithdrawRefusalState` (the one-shot flash cookie of a refused plain form post), `LinkHandler` (at `init`: follows a secret link with a 302 that drops the token and sets the signed session cookie `kalaka_active`, or the bad-link flag `kalaka_badlink`; verifies the cookie and clears a bad one; never cached when either cookie is sent; clears the cookie on logout), `Response` and `HttpResponse` (what the handler sends), `ActiveRegistrationState` (the request's resolved state), `ParticipantBlocks` (the renders the blocks and their shortcodes share, never cached, with `portal.css`; the status render builds the `WithdrawOffer`), `StatusMarkup` (the markup and texts of the two blocks; amounts through Foundation's `Api\Money\MoneyText`), `MoneyText` (an amount as `48 000 Ft`), and `LineLabels` (the label of an itemised payment line: none yet, so the table shows its totals; `owedLabelOf()`: the words of an owed row of the money table). `portal.css` is the blocks' structural stylesheet, served from `src/`.

Entry points: the blocks `Blocks/status/` (`kalaka/status`, "Jelentkezésem állapota", shortcode `[kalaka_status]`) and `Blocks/transfer/` (`kalaka/transfer`, "Átutalási adatok", shortcode `[kalaka_transfer]`), each a server render with an editor placeholder; `Blocks/status/view.js`, the status block's front-end script (the withdrawal's confirmation in a WordPress Modal, from data on the start form; the shortcode's render enqueues it too); the `init` handler of the secret link; the route `POST kalaka-portal/v1/withdraw` (`Rest\WithdrawRoute`, on `rest_api_init`); the `template_redirect` reader of the withdrawal refusal cookie (`WithdrawRefusalCookie`).
