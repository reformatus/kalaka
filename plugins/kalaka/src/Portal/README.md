# Portal

The active registration (set by a secret link or an account's pick), the participant blocks admins place on any page, and the account area.

`Api/` holds `ActiveRegistration` (`current()`: the key the verified cookie names; `followsBadLink()`: the one-shot flag of a link that did not match), `PayableNow` (the one rule of what is payable now, read by the transfer block and by starting a card payment), `StatusRead` (`payableNow()`, `of()` and `forActive()`: one registration's `RegistrationStatusView`, composed from the module Apis and the clock; it writes and evaluates nothing), `MoneyRead` (the participant's money table, `ParticipantMoney`: owed rows `OwedRow` of an `OwedKind`, movements `MovementRow` of a `MovementType` and `MovementDirection`, the totals, and `due` or `overpaid`; one ledger statement of the participant payer and the names of its owed lines, for every status) and the view's parts: `Availability`, `ConfirmationView`, `PaymentView` (the totals, the other payers, whether a request awaits an answer, and the due time; the itemised table is `MoneyRead`'s), `PayerAmounts`, `TransferDetails`. `MailMoney` is what a mail reads: the money table and the transfer block in mail markup (`MailMarkup`), and one payment line of the participant as a `MailMovement` (the `MovementRow` and its method's ID), through the same read as the page. `RegistrationStatusView::showsMoney()` is the one rule of whether a money table shows, for the page and for the mail.

At the module root: `StatusMarkup` (the page's markup, with the money table of the owner's design), `MoneyWords` (the words the page and the mail share), `MailMarkup` (the mail look: inline styles, no popover), `LinkHandler` (at `init`: follows a secret link with a 302 that drops the token and sets the signed session cookie `kalaka_active`, or the bad-link flag `kalaka_badlink`; verifies the cookie and clears a bad one; never cached when either cookie is sent; clears the cookie on logout), `Response` and `HttpResponse` (what the handler sends), `ActiveRegistrationState` (the request's resolved state), `ParticipantBlocks` (the renders the blocks and their shortcodes share, never cached, with `portal.css`), and `LineLabels` (`owedLabelOf()`: the words of an owed row of the money table). An amount's text, `MoneyText`, is Foundation's (`Foundation\Api\Money`). `portal.css` is the blocks' structural stylesheet, served from `src/`.

Entry points: the blocks `Blocks/status/` (`kalaka/status`, "Jelentkezésem állapota", shortcode `[kalaka_status]`) and `Blocks/transfer/` (`kalaka/transfer`, "Átutalási adatok", shortcode `[kalaka_transfer]`), each a server render with an editor placeholder; the `init` handler of the secret link.
