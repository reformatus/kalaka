# Ledger

Lines, payers, balances, and the organizer-recorded payments, refunds, corrections, manual charges and manual discounts.

At the module root: `LedgerLineRepository` (the only writer of `kalaka_ledger_lines`, append-only, keyed by ID and read per registration), `PaymentNoteRepository` (the only writer of `kalaka_payment_notes`, the free text of a manual line, which erasure reaches), `ManualFeeRepository` (the only writer of `kalaka_manual_fees`, the facts of the manual charges and discounts: a row is inserted once, and the erase sets its title to null), `BalanceQuery` (the balances of a registration, computed from its lines), `PaymentFactQuery` (its payment-fact lines), `SummaryQuery` (each payer's charged, paid and balance and its other lines, for display), `LineListQuery` (its lines newest first, each with its note, for display), `StatementQuery` (one payer's owed lines and movements with their notes and its totals, one statement, for display), `EntryRow` (the display reads' row as a `LedgerEntry`) and `ChargeTotalQuery` (its `charge` lines summed per source and payer, for the materializer only).

`Api/` holds `Ledger` (the contract: `record()`, `entriesOf()`, `recordManualFee()`, `manualFeesOf()`, `eraseNotesOf()`, `holdsLineKeyStartingWith()`, `balancesOf()`, `paymentFactsOf()`, `summaryOf()`, `statementOf()`, and `chargeTotalsOf()` for Pricing's materializer only, plus the per-event action names `VIEW_MONEY` and `RECORD_MONEY`), `Summary`, `PayerSummary`, `PayerStatement`, `ChargeLine`, `ChargeTotal`, `NewLine`, `RecordedLine` (what `record()` returns: the line and whether this call inserted it), `LedgerLine`, `LedgerEntry`, `LineKind`, `Side` (owed or paid: each line kind declares one), `ManualFee`, `ManualFeeType`, `RecordedManualFee`, `Payer`, `PayerKind`, `PayerKinds` (the participant and the kinds that apply to the event from the `PayerKindSource`s modules contribute; `payersOf()` and `mayRecordFor()` are the one rule for which payers a registration lists and accepts money for), `PayerKindSource` and `PayerKindInput` (the point's contract and its input: the event), `Balances`, `PaymentFact`, `PaymentMethod` and `IdempotencyConflict`.

Use cases: `RecordPayment`, `RecordRefund` and `RecordCorrection`, the manual entries (all `RecordManualEntry`: one line and its note, under the registration's lock; a payment or a refund also decides the "payment recorded" mail, a correction none). A correction (the owner's ruling 2026-11-19) is a `manual_payment` (`direction` `in`) or `manual_refund` (`out`) flagged `correction`, with a required reason, no method and no value date: it counts from the time it is recorded, and as a payment fact like any recorded payment; it needs `kalaka_record_money`.

`RecordCharge` and `RecordDiscount` (both `RecordManualFee`) record a manual charge or discount („Díj”, „Kedvezmény”) of a `registered` registration: one fact in `kalaka_manual_fees` with a required title the registrant sees, which the core `manual` fee component (Pricing) turns into its own `charge` line in the same run. Marked `completed`, the same run also records the payment (a charge) or the refund (a discount) of the same amount as a plain, unlinked entry, with one audit row and one caller key (`manual_fee:<key>`, the payment `manual_fee:<key>:paid`). One permission, `kalaka_record_money`, carries all five use cases.

A replay with the same idempotency key and the same line is no conflict, and neither is one after the line's note was erased (`eraseNotesOf()`). Since a line keeps no mark of an erased note, a replay that adds a note to a line recorded without one is accepted too, and the new note is dropped.

Tables, created by the schema in `Module.php`: `kalaka_ledger_lines`, `kalaka_payment_notes`, `kalaka_manual_fees`.

Entry points: the abilities `kalaka/record-payment`, `kalaka/record-refund`, `kalaka/record-correction`, `kalaka/record-charge` and `kalaka/record-discount`.
