# Mail

Mails, deliveries and the sender. So far: the delivery queue, the sender, pausing, the mail counts, the mail bodies, the renderer and the action-mail catalogue and the manual mail.

At the module root: `Delivery` (one row of the send log) with `DeliveryStatus`, `DeliveryRepository` (the only writer of `kalaka_deliveries`; `queue()` is an `INSERT IGNORE`) and `DeliveryQuery` (the sender's candidates, the hour's claims and the counts); the system use cases `ClaimDelivery` (`kalaka/claim-delivery`), `RecordDelivery` (`kalaka/record-delivery`, which also pauses after two errors in a row) and `FailDelivery` (`kalaka/fail-delivery`, no valid address or an unusable body), and the admin use cases `ClearMailQueue` (`kalaka/clear-mail-queue`) and `ClearEventMail` (`kalaka/clear-event-mail`, one unpublished event's held deliveries); `Sender` (the one sender, a `PeriodicStep`), `Throttle` (each run's share of the hourly budget), `Stopwatch` (the 20-second cap), `DeliveryError` (the stored error, as reported), `MailComposer` and `MailMessage` (the seam to the mail bodies) and `SendReport`.

`Pause/` holds the state row: `MailState`, `MailStateRepository`, `MailStateQuery` (the fresh read), `PauseCause`, the use cases `PauseMail` (`kalaka/pause-mail`), `ResumeMail` (`kalaka/resume-mail`) and `SetMailBudget` (`kalaka/set-mail-budget`, with its refusal `MailBudgetInvalid`), `BudgetSetting` (the hourly budget as a WordPress setting over the state row: the group `kalaka_mail` and the option `kalaka_mail_hourly_budget` for `options.php`, read from the row, saved through `SetMailBudget`, never stored as an option), and `PauseWarning` (the two site warnings, `mail_paused_after_errors` and `mail_paused`) with `RequestState`, the one read per request they share.

`Catalogue/` holds the per-event settings, with the typed refusals `MailNeedsBody`, `BodyTaken`, `PointTemplateUnpublished`, `LayoutPlaceholderCount` and `LayoutOtherPlaceholder` (`Body/` has `PasswordLinkRequired` and `RegisteredMailStaysOn`): `ActionMailEntry` and `ActionMailRepository` (the only writer of `kalaka_action_mails`), `MailBody` (a body's facts, read before the run), `MailLayout` (an event's raw HTML layout with its one `{{content}}`) and `MailLayoutRepository` (the only writer of `kalaka_mail_layouts`), and the use cases `UpdateActionMail` (`kalaka/update-action-mail`, which switches a point off when its template is assigned, swapped or unassigned) and `SetEventMailLayout` (`kalaka/set-event-mail-layout`).

`Body/` holds the mail documents and their rendering: `MailPosts` (reads the `kalaka_mail` posts; `MailDocument`), `MailRestController` (REST for full access only), `BodyInUse` (a body a switched-on action mail uses is neither trashed nor deleted, stays published and keeps its event), `BodyCheck` (a body's save check against the mail's keys), `MailValues` (a mail's value context, its declared keys and its recipient), `MailRenderer` with `RenderedMail`, `ActionMailComposer`, `ManualMailComposer` and `KindComposer`, the composer the module binds into the sender: a manual delivery is composed from its send batch, every other as an action mail. `MailValues` also declares the page-link value `page_<ID>.link`.

`Manual/` holds the manual mail: `MailBatch` and `MailBatchMember` with their repositories (the only writers of `kalaka_mail_batches` and `kalaka_mail_batch_members`), `MailBatchQuery` and the use case `SendManualMail` (`kalaka/send-manual-mail`, carried by the grant action `kalaka_manual_mail`).

`Api/` holds `MailQueue` (`add()`, `addAll()` and the nudge), `ManualMails` (`GRANT_ACTION`, the grant action's one name; `send()`: the entry of `kalaka/send-manual-mail`, which runs `SendManualMail::prepared()`, resolving a view `selection` to codes first; `preview()`: the checked preview of a `selection` (codes, or a held view with the grid's filters and search), access before the plan, with `count`; `batches()` and `batch()`: the batches' own record, with `BatchInfo`), `MailHold` (`sentEventIds()`, `isHeld()` and `mayClear()`: whether an event's pending mail is held and whether `ClearEventMail` would accept it, a read with no side effects), `MailKind`, `ActionMail` (the catalogue: four action mails), `ActionMails` (`due()`: deciding an action mail), `ActionMailSettings` (`ofEvent()`; `switchesOf()`: `can`, `refusals` and `enable_unavailable` of a switch from `UpdateActionMail::refuse()`; and `update()`: the entry of `kalaka/update-action-mail`, which runs `UpdateActionMail::prepared()`) with `EventMailSettings` and `ActionMailSetting`, `MailTemplates` (`createFor()`: a point's new template, an empty draft of the event assigned to it, the point switched off, behind `POST kalaka-admin/v1/events/{id}/mail/points/{mail}/template`; `createDraft()`: an empty draft with no point, behind `POST kalaka-admin/v1/events/{id}/mail/templates`), `MailBodies` (`ofEvent()`, `publishedOf()`, `subjects()`: the event's `kalaka_mail` posts, with `MailBodyInfo`), `Deliveries` (`troubled()`, with `TroubledDelivery`), `AccountMail` (`modeProblem()`: whether the "registered" mail can back an account mode), and `MailStats` (`site()`, `eventIds()`, `ofEvents()`) with `SiteMailStats`, `EventMailStats` and `PauseInfo`, and `StarterTemplate` (the code-defined subject and body of an event's first mail; the new-mail action offers it).

`Rest/` holds `MailRoutes`, the six reads of the mail screens under `kalaka-admin/v1` (an event's mail page, the sender, an event's troubled deliveries, its batches and one batch, and the manual mail's checked preview) and the two writes: `POST …/events/{id}/mail/points/{mail}/template`, a point's new draft template, and `POST …/events/{id}/mail/templates`, one with no point.

Tables, created by the schema in `Module.php`: `kalaka_deliveries`, `kalaka_mail_states`, `kalaka_action_mails`, `kalaka_mail_layouts`, `kalaka_mail_batches` and `kalaka_mail_batch_members`. Post type: `kalaka_mail`, with the meta `kalaka_event_id`; the module contributes a `BlockHost` so its editor offers the condition blocks (`Module::BODY_BLOCKS`).

`Cli/` holds `MailCommand`, `wp kalaka mail status|pause|resume|budget|clear-queue|clear-event|failed`, over the five Pause, Budget and Clear use cases (`resume` needs `--user`; `budget <n>` passes its argument as given to `SetMailBudget`; the clears ask or take `--yes`; `failed <event_id>` lists the registration codes of an event's failed, errored and stuck deliveries with their stored error), `ManualMailCommand` and `ManualMailBatchesCommand`, `wp kalaka mail send|batches` (`send <event_id> --body=<post_id> --codes=<a,b>|--codes-file=<path> [--key=<token>] [--dry-run] [--yes]` queues one send batch; `batches <event_id> [<batch_id>]` lists them), and `ActionMailCommand`, `wp kalaka action-mail list|show|on|off|body|layout <event_id> …`, over the two use cases of the catalogue: `body` and `layout` take their content from `--file`, and a first body needs its subject, `--title`.

Entry points: the WP-Cron hook `kalaka_send_mail` (the nudge; the plugin's deactivation clears it) and the periodic run's step `mail_sender`. The use cases are abilities (`Module::boot()`), the reads are `Rest\MailRoutes`, and WP-CLI is the other entry point.
